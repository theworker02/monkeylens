# MonkeyLens brand

The mark combines a magnifying lens, method-chain strokes, and a Ruby-shaped pupil. It represents inspection of Ruby's effective dispatch path.

## Assets

- `assets/brand/monkeylens-mark.svg` — square icon and avatar.
- `assets/brand/monkeylens-lockup.svg` — README and presentation lockup.

## Palette

- Ink: `#111827`
- Ruby: `#dc2626`
- Lens orange: `#f97316`
- Highlight: `#fdba74`
- Slate: `#64748b`

Keep clear space around the mark equal to one quarter of its diameter. Do not recolor individual geometry, skew the mark, or place it on visually noisy backgrounds.
